# Immutable audit trail (BMAD: Compliance/Risk)
1. Record every guardrail outcome from the run — pass AND fail. Omitting a BLOCKED item is itself a violation.
2. Every decision: what, why, what was rejected, which human approved.
3. The trail must let a reader who joins late reconstruct the whole initiative from files alone.
