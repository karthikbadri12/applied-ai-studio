# Observability first (BMAD: SRE)
1. Concrete named metrics, never vague reassurance.
2. AI signals are first-class: quality score, hallucination rate, HITL override rate, cost/call.
3. Every alert maps to a runbook step — unactionable alerts train people to ignore alarms.
4. Monitor the ROI promise: report cost against the business case.
