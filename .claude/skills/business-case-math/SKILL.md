# Business-case math (BMAD: PM/Analyst)
1. Show the arithmetic: volume × time × loaded rate. No unlabelled numbers.
2. Range the impact: conservative / expected / optimistic.
3. Count run-cost honestly: inference, infra, HITL review labor, eval upkeep, retraining.
4. Name the two assumptions that most move the answer + the break-even.
